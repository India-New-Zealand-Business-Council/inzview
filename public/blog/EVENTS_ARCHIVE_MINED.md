# Archive events mined from the remaining posts (2020–2025)

Second pass over the ~137 posts not caught by the first Date:/Time:/Venue: regex
(see `EVENTS_EXTRACTED.md` for those 17). These didn't use that exact label format, but
are real events — dates and venues pulled from body text and titles instead. Pure news/press
releases (FTA signings, board appointments, policy updates) were excluded even though some
carry a date, since they aren't things to list as an "event" on the Events page.

Two groups: **A** — INZBC's own events (summits, delegations, panels, workshops it ran or
co-ran); **B** — third-party India trade shows/expos INZBC circulated to members (real
events, but INZBC didn't host them — useful for a "trade opportunities" list more than an
"our events" list). Cover images NOT downloaded for these yet (48 posts) — say if you want
them pulled like the other two batches.
