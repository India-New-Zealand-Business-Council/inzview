# Event photo provenance

Real INZBC event photography, not stock substitutes. Where a photo came from matters as much
as what it shows — see HANDOVER.md's sourcing rules.

## 7 October 2026 batch

Sunil Kaushal (CEO, INZBC) forwarded a folder of photos by email, shared as a Google Drive
folder ("Website photos") to the account working on this repo. Downloaded directly from that
folder on 7–8 October 2026, resized for web (longest edge 1400–1800px, JPEG ~82% quality) and
placed here under new, descriptive filenames. Captions and event names used in the site come
from what's legible on the photos themselves (official INZBC title slides and speaker-name
graphics), not invented.
